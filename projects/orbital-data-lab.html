Data and Simulation Evidence

Orbital Data Lab

A deterministic, educational two-body RK4 and velocity-Verlet comparison with validated scenario storage and exports. It is explicitly not flight grade.

Evidence snapshot

Source: orbital-data-lab

Run from repository root: Set-Location orbital-data-lab; python -m uvicorn orbital_lab.api:app --port 8010

Validation: Circular-orbit, energy drift, determinism, bounded input, API, idempotent storage, lineage, and export tests.

Implemented

  • Configurable central body label, gravity parameter, initial state, duration, step size, and integrators
  • Trajectory samples, browser visualization, energy drift and final-state comparison
  • Content-addressed SQLite scenarios, share paths, lineage metadata, and JSON/CSV export

Limitations

The point-mass model omits perturbations, atmosphere, burns, ephemerides, frames, uncertainty, and collision handling. It is not suitable for navigation or safety decisions.
